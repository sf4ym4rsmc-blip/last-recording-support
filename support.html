How do I switch languages?

Open the globe menu, marked 中文 / EN, on the home screen or immersive investigation panel. Choose System default, 简体中文 or English. English mode uses English dialogue; Chinese mode uses Mandarin. Switching does not reset your case.

How do I play?

Look at an object and pinch to inspect it, or use the floating case-file panel. With optional hand tracking, bring your hand close to a document or receiver, pinch to hold it, turn your wrist and release to set it down. You can finish the entire story in the panel without hand tracking.

No audio?

Check the app's Mute control and system volume. Story audio is bundled and does not need a network connection. Replay key recordings from the recorder or ending panel.

Need a hint or a fresh start?

Use Hint in the investigation panel. Progress is saved locally. Restart asks for confirmation before clearing the current case. Reinstalling can remove your local progress.

语言：在首页或沉浸调查面板的“中文 / EN”地球菜单中选择跟随系统、简体中文或 English。中文播放普通话，英文模式播放英文，切换不清除存档。

操作：注视物件并捏合即可检查；授权手部跟踪后，可近手捏合抓取、转动手腕翻看、松手放下。未授权也可在调查面板完成故事。

声音：检查静音按钮与系统音量。全部剧情音频随应用提供，无需联网。录音机和结局面板可重播对白。

进度自动保存在本机。卡关可用“提示”；重新开始需确认并会清除本次调查。删除应用会清除本地进度。

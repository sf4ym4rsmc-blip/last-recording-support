How do I place the tree?

Choose a mode and difficulty, then Place Tree. Preparation shows the current step and elapsed time. Allow World Sensing for automatic floor alignment. Face your chosen spot, tap Place in Front, check the live preview and select Place Here & Play. Presets and Fine-tune controls adjust distance, tree height and floor height.

Placement seems slow or roots float

Slowly look around a well-lit room and toward a textured floor. Estimated floor placement is explicitly labeled and can be adjusted in Fine-tune. If headset position is unavailable, use Retry spatial tracking or return to the main menu and re-enter. World Sensing can be managed in system privacy settings.

How do I aim, shoot and zoom?

Turn your head to center the reticle on a target, then pinch your thumb and index finger. Use the controls beside the rifle to reload, pause or switch between 3× and 6× magnification. No game controller is required.

What does the shepherd do?

The shepherd sits while waiting, stands and chases grounded birds, feeds, returns and sits again. Its state appears in the HUD. The companion can be disabled in settings. Reduce Motion turns off dust effects.

No sound?

Check system volume, Audio enabled and the music, effects and ambience sliders. Dog barks, steps and feeding follow Weapon & Companion Effects. Audio is bundled with the app; it does not require streaming.

Language, scores and purchases

怎样放置大树？

选择模式和难度，点“放置大树”。准备过程中会显示真实步骤与等待时间。允许空间感知后可以自动贴合地面；面向想放树的位置，点“放在我前方”，检查预览，再点“就放这里·开始游戏”。预设和微调可实时调整距离、树高与地面高度。

定位慢，或树根悬空怎么办？

保持室内光线充足，缓慢看看四周与有纹理的地面。使用估算地面时会明确提示，可在“微调位置”调整地面高度。无法定位时点“重新识别空间”，或返回大厅再进入。空间感知权限可在系统隐私设置中管理。

射击与瞄准镜

转头让中央准星对准目标，捏合拇指与食指开枪。枪旁控制区可换弹、暂停，或切换 3 倍 / 6 倍放大。不需要额外手柄。

德牧与声音

德牧会坐着等候，起身追逐落鸟、低头进食，再返回原位。HUD 显示当前动作，设置中可以关闭伙伴。检查系统音量、“音乐与音效”开关及各音量滑杆；狗叫、脚步和进食声属于“枪械与伙伴音效”。声音随应用提供，无需在线播放。“减少动态效果”会关闭脚边扬尘。

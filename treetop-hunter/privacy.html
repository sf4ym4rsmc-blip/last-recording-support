PRIVACY / UPDATED 21 OCTOBER 2026

Your room stays yours.

Privacy policy for Treetop Hunter, the native Apple Vision Pro game developed by Beijing Yimo Bufan Film And Television Culture Media Co., Ltd.

Data stays on your device

The app requires no account and includes no advertising, tracking or analytics SDK. The developer does not collect or receive player data from the app. Language, audio and accessibility preferences, companion settings and high scores are stored locally using system storage. The app has no developer-operated game backend.

World Sensing and interaction

With permission, visionOS provides floor plane information to align the virtual tree. Headset pose is used transiently to place content and aim. The game does not save or upload these tracking values, floor maps or room images. It uses system passthrough to show your room; it does not capture a recording of that view. The game does not request microphone or camera-recording access. Declining World Sensing allows estimated-floor placement when headset tracking is available.

Audio, purchases and system services

Music, bird calls, dog sounds and weapon audio are bundled for playback. The app does not record your voice. Apple handles App Store purchases, payments and refunds under its own policies; the developer does not receive payment-card details. Diagnostics that you choose to share with Apple follow the system settings and Apple's policies.

Your controls

Change audio, language, companion and accessibility preferences in the game. Manage World Sensing permission in system settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data according to platform storage behavior. There is no developer account or server-side player profile to delete.

Support and this website

If you email 553748912@qq.com, we receive what you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not send unnecessary personal information. This website adds no advertising or analytics scripts. Its hosting service may process technical request information needed to deliver pages; this is separate from the native game.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Contact: 553748912@qq.com.

隐私政策 · 更新于 2026 年 10 月 21 日

你的房间信息，留在你的设备里。

本政策适用于北京一墨不凡影视文化传媒有限公司开发的 Apple Vision Pro 原生游戏《树梢猎手》。

本地数据

游戏无需账号，不含广告、追踪或分析 SDK。开发者不从应用收集或接收玩家数据。语言、音量、辅助功能、德牧开关和最高分通过系统存储保存在本机。游戏没有开发者运营的游戏服务器。

空间感知与交互

授权后，visionOS 提供地面平面信息，用于放置大树；运行时使用头显位置和朝向来放置内容与瞄准。这些跟踪数据、地面地图和房间图像不保存、不上传。现实房间由系统透视显示，游戏不录制透视画面，也不申请麦克风或相机录像权限。拒绝空间感知后，头显定位可用时仍可使用估算地面放置。

音频、购买与系统服务

音乐、鸟鸣、狗叫、脚步和枪械声音随应用提供，游戏不录制你的声音。下载、支付和退款由 Apple 按其政策处理，开发者不接收银行卡资料。你选择分享给 Apple 的系统诊断遵循系统设置和苹果政策。

管理与删除

可在游戏内管理声音、语言、伙伴和辅助功能，在系统设置管理空间感知权限。删除应用会按平台存储规则删除本地应用数据。本游戏没有开发者账号或服务器玩家档案。

技术支持与本网站

主动向 553748912@qq.com 发邮件时，我们会收到你选择发送的信息，仅用于回复支持请求；请勿发送不必要的个人信息。本网站不添加广告或分析脚本，网页托管服务可能处理页面传输所需的技术请求信息，这与原生游戏的数据处理分开。

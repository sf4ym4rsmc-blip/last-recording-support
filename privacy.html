Privacy policy

This policy describes Last Recording, the native Apple Vision Pro app developed by Beijing Yimo Bufan Film And Television Culture Media Co., Ltd.

Data stays on your device

The app requires no account and has no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. We do not collect or receive data from the app. Case progress, hint count, first-use guide status and language preference are stored locally using system storage. They are not sent to a developer server.

Optional hand tracking

With your permission, visionOS hand tracking provides pinches and wrist direction for grabbing and turning objects. This data is used transiently during play, is not saved and is not uploaded. You can decline permission and complete the story using system look-and-pinch controls and the case-file panel. The app does not request microphone or camera-recording permission.

Audio, purchases and system services

AI-generated Chinese and English dialogue, music and sound effects are bundled for offline playback. The app does not record your voice. Apple handles the App Store purchase and payment information under its own privacy policy; the developer does not receive payment-card details. System diagnostics you choose to share with Apple follow Apple's settings and policies.

Control and deletion

Restart clears the current investigation. You can change language in the app and manage hand-tracking permission in system settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data. There is no developer account or server-side player profile to delete.

Support and this website

If you email 553748912@qq.com, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information. This support website's hosting provider may process request data needed to deliver its pages; that is separate from the offline native app. This site adds no advertising or analytics scripts.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if app behavior changes. Questions: 553748912@qq.com.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日。适用于北京一墨不凡影视文化传媒有限公司开发的 Apple Vision Pro 原生应用《最后一段录音》。

数据保留在本机

无需账号，不含广告、追踪或分析 SDK。我们不从应用收集或接收数据。调查进度、提示次数、首次引导状态及语言设置仅保存在本机，不传输至开发者服务器。

可选手部跟踪

授权后使用 visionOS 系统手部跟踪识别捏合与手腕方向，仅在运行时抓取和翻看物件，不保存、不上传。拒绝授权仍可使用系统注视捏合和调查面板完成剧情。应用不请求麦克风或相机录像权限。

音频与系统服务

中英文 AI 角色配音、音乐和音效随应用提供，可离线播放；不录制您的声音。购买及付款信息由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理，开发者不会收到付款卡资料。您选择向 Apple 分享的系统诊断依照系统设置和 Apple 政策处理。

控制与删除

重新开始清除当前调查进度。语言可在应用内切换，手部跟踪权限可在系统设置管理。删除应用可清除本地应用数据。我们没有玩家账号或服务器端用户资料。
